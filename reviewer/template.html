Welcome, future attorney

This reviewer gathers case digests for the 2027 Bar, arranged by a powerful AI model from the full text of each decision. It is unofficial: it does not come from the Supreme Court or the Office of the Bar Chairperson.

Use it as one tool among many, next to your codals, your review notes, and the decisions themselves. Please don't make it your only reviewer. When a case really matters, open the decision; every digest links to it.
